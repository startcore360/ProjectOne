### 3.2 Findings that affect security

- **V4-F-17 (High, corrected).** The V3.3 schema accepted the Principal as the approver of the Principal's own leave. Go-live (C7) would pass, and every request would then be unapprovable (blocked at decision time). Fixed by V4-DB-01; DB-77 fails without the fix and passes with it.
- **V4-F-31 (High, corrected) / V4-F-32 (Medium, accepted).** A standalone institution had no role allowed to activate it, and nothing said who acts for a missing role. Product Owner decision V4-PD-01 adds the role roll-down; the Governing Body Approver gains one right (changing roll-down) only while no Management exists, enforced in the database (DB-96).
- **V4-F-16 (Low, accepted for the artefacts).** Design system and prototype load fonts from Google Fonts. The product must self-host fonts (privacy; strict Content-Security-Policy).
- **Application security is designed, not demonstrated.** Object-level authorisation, function / property-level authorisation, rate limits (OD-77), SSRF-safe callbacks, file scanning, secrets management, session controls and two-step login are specified (Deliverable 1 V4-11.2, Deliverable 5 §3) and must be proven by the application test suites and a penetration test before launch.
- **Open security decisions:** OD-03 institution RLS, OD-09 audit-store integrity, OD-10 / OD-80 identity model, OD-53 field encryption, OD-58 search isolation.

### 3.3 Privacy and Indian regulatory position (requires counsel)

The DPDP Act 2023 and DPDP Rules 2025 were notified on 13 November 2025 with phased commencement (main obligations about 18 months later, ≈ 13 May 2027). In January 2026 MeitY consulted on shortening some deadlines; in the sources consulted on 30 October 2026 this was still a proposal. EDU 360 processes children's personal data, so verifiable parental consent, the Rules' provisions for educational institutions, breach-notification timelines and retention (OD-06, OD-14) must be mapped provision by provision before launch. Board and regulator rules (CBSE attendance eligibility, UGC credit framework) are configurable per institution. **No claim of legal compliance is made.** Sources: Deliverable 1, V4-18.

### C1 Addendum — Controls and validation boundaries

C1 preserves the existing risk assessment and adds these explicit implementation controls: (1) scope/tenant validation must occur in the service layer and PostgreSQL RLS; (2) template-derived roles must create draft role configurations only; (3) one active role/assignment context must be bound into each request and revalidated on context switch; (4) approval action authority must be checked against the exact approval chain step; (5) export/download of sensitive data must be action-gated and audited; (6) support access must be tenant-granted, ticket-bound and automatically time-expired; (7) logs must avoid secrets and unnecessary student/guardian sensitive data.

**Validation boundary:** the supplied 59 DB tests are reported as passing in the supplied test log, but were not rerun in C1. No deployed application/API, browser, penetration, dependency scan, load test, backup recovery test or UAT evidence was included. Treat all application/security assessments as design recommendations and risks until executed against the implemented product.
